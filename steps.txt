Terminal 1: The Brain (FastAPI)

Open a terminal and navigate to your folder: cd path\to\shadow-ai-guard

Activate the environment: venv\Scripts\activate

Start the server: uvicorn main:app --reload
(Keep this running. It handles your database and video forensics).


Terminal 2: The Eyes (Shadow AI Proxy)

Open a second terminal and navigate to the folder: cd path\to\shadow-ai-guard

Activate the environment: venv\Scripts\activate

Start the interceptor: mitmdump -s proxy.py -p 8080
(Keep this running. It is actively watching for AI traffic).


Terminal 3: The Command Center

Open a third terminal. You don't need to activate the venv here.

We will use this terminal to run our React frontend and fire test curl commands.

Verify both Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 are running without errors. Once they are humming, we enter Phase 3.
